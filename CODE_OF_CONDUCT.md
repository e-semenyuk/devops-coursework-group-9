# Group 9 Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This agreement defines how Group 9 will collaborate on the SET09803 DevOps coursework. It applies to meetings, Microsoft Teams, GitHub, code reviews, and all project artefacts.

## Team values

We will communicate respectfully, assume good intent, share knowledge, raise blockers early, and give every member a fair opportunity to contribute.

## Communication and meetings

- Microsoft Teams is the primary discussion channel; GitHub Issues and Pull Requests hold decisions about deliverable work.
- Meeting times will state the Edinburgh time zone and, where useful, the equivalent times for remote members.
- Members should acknowledge assigned work and communicate delays or blockers as soon as practical.
- Decisions made during calls will be summarised in writing so absent members can participate.

## Work allocation and accountability

- Work is assigned through GitHub Issues with clear acceptance criteria.
- Tasks should reflect members' strengths while allowing everyone to learn.
- Each member is responsible for work attributed to their GitHub account and must understand what they submit.
- Contribution will be assessed by quality and traceability, not by manufacturing equal commit counts.
- If a member needs help, they should ask early; the team will pair, review, or redistribute work when necessary.

## Git and review practices

- Changes are made on issue-linked branches and proposed through Pull Requests.
- At least one other member reviews a Pull Request before merge.
- Members will not overwrite another person's work, rewrite shared history, or commit credentials and personal data.
- AI-assisted work must comply with university rules, be reviewed by its author, and be explainable by the submitting team member.

## Scrum and decisions

- The Product Owner maintains priorities and acceptance criteria.
- The Scrum Master facilitates the workflow, records impediments, and supports the team; the role does not own all project work.
- Technical and scope decisions should seek consensus. If consensus cannot be reached, the Product Owner decides scope/priority and the team records the rationale.

## Conflict resolution

Concerns should first be discussed privately and respectfully with the people involved. If unresolved, the Scrum Master facilitates a team discussion. Serious or continuing academic, behavioural, or participation concerns may be escalated to the module leader with a factual record.
